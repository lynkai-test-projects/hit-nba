# NBA Context

## NBA Overview

The National Basketball Association (NBA) is the premier professional basketball league in North America, consisting of 30 teams divided into Eastern and Western Conferences. Each conference has three divisions. The NBA season runs from October through June, with the regular season followed by playoffs and the NBA Finals.

### Season Structure

- **Regular Season**: Typically 82 games per team (varies historically), running from October to April
- **Playoffs**: Post-season elimination tournament featuring the top teams from each conference
- **NBA Finals**: Best-of-seven championship series between conference champions
- **Pre-Season**: Exhibition games before the regular season
- **All-Star**: Mid-season exhibition events including the All-Star Game

### Key NBA Concepts

- **Seasons**: Identified by the ending year (e.g., 2023-24 season is referenced as "2024 season")
- **Games**: Each game has two teams (home and away), with comprehensive statistics tracked
- **Players**: Active and historical players with career statistics and biographical data
- **Teams**: NBA franchises, including historical relocations and name changes
- **Coaches**: Head coaches and assistant coaches who manage teams

## Data Coverage
- **Data Coverage Period**: NBA season context is available for all seasons from 1947 through 2023. Data for seasons outside this range (i.e., before 1947 or after 2023) is not covered.
- **Historical Depth**: Complete statistical records since 1970s
- **Temporal Scope**: Data from NBA founding (1946) through 2023
- **Coverage**: All NBA games, players, teams, and coaches
- **Completeness**: Some metrics may have limited historical coverage; franchise moves and name changes are tracked historically
